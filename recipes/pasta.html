Pasta For Everyone!


DESCRIPTION

Welcome to pasta for everyone people! Creamy, rich, and packed with flavor, this pasta recipe is the perfect comfort meal for any time of the day.
Fresh ingredients, aromatic herbs, and a velvety sauce come together in every bite.
Easy to make and irresistibly delicious, it’s ideal for quick dinners or cozy gatherings.
Serve it hot with garlic bread or cheese on top for the ultimate pasta experience.

Ingredients

  • Semolina or Durum wheat flour
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Butter Or Olive Oil
  • Parmigiano cheese
  • garlic
  • Herbs
  • Crushed Red Pepper
  • Milk
  • Cream

Steps:

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil, add salt, and cook the pasta until al dente (usually 7-8 mins). Drain and keep aside, reserving 1/4 cup of pasta
  2. In a pan, melt 1/2 tbsp butter and sauté vegetables until slightly tender yet crunchy (about 5 mins). Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same pan, melt 2 tbsp butter over medium heat, add minced garlic, and sauté until fragrant (30-40 seconds).
  4. Add the all-purpose flour and stir constantly for 1 minute until fragrant but not browned
  5. Gradually pour in the milk while whisking constantly to prevent lumps. Simmer for 2-3 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon.
  6. Add salt, pepper, herbs, and cheese/cream (if using). Mix in the sautéed vegetables and boiled pasta.
  7. Cook for another 1-2 minutes until the pasta is coated. If too thick, add a splash of the reserved pasta water

Tips For Success

  • Prevent Lumps: Whisk continuously when adding milk to the roux.
  • Prevent Dry Pasta: Keep the sauce slightly thinner than desired, as it will thicken upon cooling.
  • Vegetable Crunch: Sauté vegetables separately to ensure they remain crunchy.
